# MadjosBuilds — Low-Refund Product Ideas

tags: [gumroad, passive-income, madjosbuilds, ideas, low-risk]

## Why Pure Info Products = No Refunds
Code products get "it doesn't work" complaints. PDF/template/doc products don't "break" — 
there's nothing to install, nothing to debug. Refund rate drops to near zero.

---

## Tier A — Boss's Core Expertise (Salesforce + AI)

### 1. Salesforce Apex & LWC Developer Interview Kit 🥇
- **Price**: $24-29
- **Format**: PDF (60-80 pages)
- **Content**: 50+ Apex Q&A, 30+ LWC questions, Agentforce/AI cert prep, Revenue Cloud/CPQ, cheat sheet
- **Edge**: Authentic Accenture Platform App Manager — not generic GPT content
- **Market**: Salesforce devs prepping for interviews, promotions, cert exams
- **Refund risk**: Near zero — it's study material

### 2. Salesforce AI Associate Certification Study Pack
- **Price**: $19-24
- **Format**: PDF + flashcard-style Markdown
- **Content**: Full cert syllabus coverage, practice questions, scenario walkthroughs
- **Edge**: Boss already has the cert — writes from experience
- **Market**: ~100K Salesforce devs chasing the AI Associate cert right now

### 3. Agentforce Implementation Cheat Sheet
- **Price**: $9-14
- **Format**: 1-page PDF (printable reference card) — low price, high volume potential
- **Content**: Agentforce Agent Types, Action Types, Topics, guardrails, limits, quick-start recipe
- **Edge**: Perfect impulse buy — cheap, fast to make, practical
- **Market**: Any Salesforce admin/dev who touches Agentforce
