### 3 Working Pipelines Included

**1. Research Assistant**  
Ask a question → ResearcherAgent searches the web + synthesizes → WriterAgent produces a clean report → saved to file.

**2. Blog Post Generator**  
Give a topic → Research + Write pipeline → Full 600-900 word blog post saved as Markdown.

**3. Multi-Agent Orchestrator**  
Give a high-level goal → Orchestrator auto-plans → Delegates to Researcher / Writer / Coder → Full output saved.
